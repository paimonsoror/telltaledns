Performance

Numbers from the project's own benchmark harness (make bench-full), on loopback, with the raw results one click away.

How to read this: peak is the most queries per second the server answered with no loss while dnsperf pushed as hard as it could on the same machine; latency at peak is mostly queueing in the load generator. The at 25 / 50 / 75% load rows replay a fixed rate below that peak: that's what devices on a home network experience. cache-hot asks 10,000 names repeatedly (answers from the cache), blocked asks names on the blocklists (answered by the filter, about 2.7 million names loaded), and miss-heavy sends unique names at a fixed 2,000 per second, each one resolved through a local stub upstream. Harness details: bench/README.md.
