The first stable release of TelltaleDNS: a filtering, encrypted, observable DNS resolver that runs on a Raspberry Pi, in Kubernetes, or on both as one cluster.

## Highlights

- **Fast and small.** Every CPU core answers on its own, with no garbage-collection pauses; cache hits take about 150 ns. About 85 MB of memory on a Pi 4 with 2.7 million blocked names (this said 60 MB at release; corrected 2026-10-07 from a measurement, see docs/v1-gate.md).
- **Blocking you can explain.** Hosts files, AdBlock-style lists, and regexes are compiled in the background into one compact snapshot. Every blocked answer says which list and line decided it, in the query log, the API, and `telltale explain`.
- **Groups for your networks.** Each VLAN or set of devices gets its own lists, blocked services, safe search, schedules, rewrites, DNS64, and upstream servers. Every chart and log line shows which group a question came from.
- **Encrypted both ways.** DoT, DoH (HTTP/2 and HTTP/3), and DoQ for your devices; DoT, DoH, DoQ, DNSCrypt, and DNS stamps to upstreams; DNSSEC validation, aggressive NSEC (RFC 8198), or full recursion from the root.
- **Observable.** Per-question timing and percentiles, a searchable columnar query log, a live view across the cluster, Prometheus metrics with a Grafana dashboard, device anomaly detection, alerts (email, ntfy, Gotify, Slack, webhooks), and exports to syslog, OpenTelemetry, and dnstap.
- **One cluster, one place to manage it.** A Pi and Kubernetes nodes form one cluster with replicated configuration, write forwarding, automatic failover, and configuration from Git.
- **Ready for AI agents.** Scoped agent tokens or SSO, an MCP server whose changes come as plans you approve, and vqlog for analytics in one line.
- **Easy to try.** Brings over a Pi-hole or Technitium setup; installs as a container, a Helm chart, or a native systemd service with a guided installer (and `--uninstall`).

## Install

- **Container:** `ghcr.io/paimonsoror/telltale:0.1.0` (also `:0.1`, `:0`, `:latest`) for amd64, arm64, and arm/v7.
- **Kubernetes:** `helm install telltale oci://ghcr.io/paimonsoror/charts/telltale --version 0.1.0`
- **Raspberry Pi or Linux:** `curl -fsSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/paimonsoror/telltaledns/v0.1.0/deploy/systemd/install.sh && sudo sh install.sh`

Binaries are signed: `SHA256SUMS.minisig` verifies with the key in `deploy/release/telltale-release.pub`, and `install.sh` and `telltale self-update` check it for you.

## Upgrading from edge builds

Nothing to change in your configuration. Native installs on the edge channel can stay there (`telltale self-update --channel edge`) or move to stable (`telltale self-update`). Container and Helm users can switch from `:edge` to a version tag.
