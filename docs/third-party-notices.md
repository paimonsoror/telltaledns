# Third-party notices

## Domain icons (the web UI)
The brand marks in `ui/src/lib/icons/brands.ts` come from two packs. [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) 16.38.0 is the first choice; it is released under the CC0 1.0 Universal public domain dedication. For the marks Simple Icons has dropped (Microsoft, Amazon, LinkedIn, Slack, OpenAI, and a few more) the glyphs come from [Bootstrap Icons](https://icons.getbootstrap.com) 1.13.2, released under the MIT licence below, given a brand colour by hand. Simple Icons asks everyone who uses its marks to read its disclaimer: the marks are trademarks or logos of their owners, and are shown here only to identify the name they belong to. TelltaleDNS is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

The glyph data and the map from domains to brands are generated by `ui/scripts/brand-icons.mjs` and committed, so a build never fetches anything (the path data is minified with [svgo](https://github.com/svg/svgo) 4.1.0, MIT, while it is generated; svgo is not shipped). To add a brand or move to another release of either pack, change the list or the pinned version in that script and run it (the comment at its top says how, and the developer page [Extend the domain icons](https://paimonsoror.github.io/telltaledns/dev-icons.html) has the whole recipe), then review the diff.
